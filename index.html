Our Philosophy

Assist, Don't Replace

JointSense works alongside existing clinical methods — amplifying a doctor's capability, not competing with it.

7 sensor modalities capturing gait, crepitus, ROM, EMG, temperature, symptoms, and posture — data that's impossible to quantify with a stethoscope.

Data Fusion Engine

How Sensor + Clinical Data Merge

The AI fusion model combines 4 automated sensor channels with 12 manual clinical features for a multi-modal composite risk assessment.

Automated Sensor Data 4 channels

  • IMU gait kinematics (stride, asymmetry, stance)
  • Crepitus Angle Map — novel biomarker
  • Flex sensor ROM (flexion, extension deficit)
  • sEMG AMI detection (VMO:VL ratio, onset)
  • TMII — inflammation phenotype index
  • IR bilateral temperature ΔT
  • Camera pose — varus/valgus angle
→

Manual Clinical Data 12 features

  • Age, BMI, gender, occupation
  • Previous knee injury history
  • Morning stiffness duration
  • KOOS-12 subscale scores
  • Joint tenderness (0–3 scale)
  • Effusion grade, bony enlargement
  • Prior X-Ray KL grade, lab results
→
